    The road to the platinum

    Four stages, in order. Finish the story first, then the side content, then the grinds — the collectibles and the 100% checklist come last, when the whole map is open.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy on the whole list can be missed. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam.

    About this guide

    Leonida Ledger is a free, open-source, offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. It runs entirely from its own files — no network, no account, no tracking. Your progress is saved in this browser only (localStorage), and you can export it to a file and import it back.

    Where the facts come from

    • Trophy names, descriptions, grades and counts as PlayStation publishes them: the Exophase trophy list, cross-checked with PSNProfiles.
    • How-to guidance is written in this guide's own words for this entry, and each trophy's guide names its sources beside it. No guide text is copied from another guide.
    • Where a location or step could not be pinned down from the official descriptions, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one.

    Sources used

    • Exophase — GTA VI trophy list (names, descriptions, grades, rarity)
    • PSNProfiles — GTA VI trophies (counts, cross-check)
    • KeenGamer — GTA 6 trophy guide & platinum roadmap (missable warning, grind counts)
